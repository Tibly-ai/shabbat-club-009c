Best Weekend Shabbat Dinners and Holiday Events in Manhattan

Fridays are for friends. If you're looking for the best Shabbat dinners, holiday parties, and Jewish social events happening in Manhattan this weekend, Shabbat Club runs a culturally Jewish pop-up dinner party series and community across NYC — long tables, candles, loud rooms, and a room full of people who mostly didn't know each other at 7pm.

This isn't a synagogue membership model. There's no ticket line and no dues. Every seat at every event is applied for, through that specific event's own application link. This page is where you find out what's actually running this weekend and how to get on the list for it.

  • Museum tours
  • Volunteer trips
  • Summer Sessions
  • A three-day adult summer camp in the Poconos

How to Get In This Weekend

There are two steps:

  1. Apply to the general interest list — this gets you on Shabbat Club's list so they know you exist and what you're interested in (Shabbat dinners, Almost Shabbat, Shvitz Club, karaoke, Summer Sessions, holiday parties, or the Poconos camp weekend).
  2. Apply again, per event — once you're on the list, you receive the individual application link for that specific Friday dinner or holiday party. That link is where the real event application happens.

Shabbat Club reads every form themselves — there's no algorithm sorting applicants, and a human reaches out when something fits.
