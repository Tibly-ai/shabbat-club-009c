Three ways in

Shabbat Club is a culturally Jewish pop-up dinner party series and community in NYC. Seats at individual events are handled through each event's own application link. Everything else starts here.

01

Apply to join in

Fill out the interest form and you land on our list. That is how we know to tell you about Friday dinners, Almost Shabbat happy hours, Shvitz Club, karaoke, Summer Sessions, and the camp weekend in the Poconos.

02

Partner or work with us

Brands, restaurants, chefs, DJs, photographers, venues, and volunteer organizers: pick "Work with us" on the form and tell us what you have in mind. You can also write directly to hello@shabbatclub.com or call (212) 555-0147.
