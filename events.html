What we run

Six ways to spend a night with us

Some nights are candles, challah and a seated table for strangers who leave as friends. Others are a steam room, a microphone or a bus to the Poconos. Here is every format, with the dates attached to it.

01  Shabbat dinner parties

The Friday table

The heart of the club. Candles, a blessing, one long table and a menu built around whoever is cooking that week. Seats are limited and assigned, so you sit next to someone new by design.

  • Jan 16Safta's Table
  • Feb 13Love Club Shabbat Dinner Party + Afters
  • May 1A Very Schmaltzy Shabbat Club

02  Almost Shabbat

Happy hours and late nights

No seating chart, no dinner course. A bar, a room and a few hundred people who found their way here through the same group chat. Come after work, stay as long as the playlist holds.

  • Mar 26Almost Shabbat: Red Room
  • Jul 25Tel Aviv Saturday Night Social

03  Shvitz Club

Steam, then snacks

An old ritual with a new crowd. We take over the bathhouse, sweat it out together and finish with something to eat. Phones stay in the locker, which is most of the point.

  • Jan 28Shvitz Club: Dry Jan Edition

04  Karaoke nights

The private room

One rented room, a queue of songs nobody should attempt, and a tambourine that changes hands all night. Talent is not a requirement. Volume is.

  • Feb 19Shabbat Club Karaoke

05  Holidays

Purim to Rosh Hashanah

We host the whole calendar: a Purim dinner party in costume, a Passover Seder with our own Haggadah, Hanukkah candle lighting and a Rosh Hashanah table. Bring your family or come make one.

  • Mar 7Shabbat Club Purim
  • Apr 21Israel Independence Day

06  Summer Sessions & camp

Out of the city

Rooftops and backyards all summer, then the big one: Wet Hot Hebrew Summer, a full camp weekend in the Poconos with bunks, a lake and Friday night dinner under the trees. Applications open early.

  • May 27Summer Sessions
  • Jun 25Summer Sessions II
  • Aug 12Summer Sessions III: Love Club
  • Sep 4–6Wet Hot Hebrew Summer, Poconos
