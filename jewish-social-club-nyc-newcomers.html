Best Jewish Social Clubs for Meeting People After Moving to NYC

You moved to New York with a job, an apartment, and maybe one friend from college who's already busy on Fridays. Shabbat Club is where a lot of people in that exact spot end up finding their group chat.

We're a culturally Jewish pop-up dinner party series and community based in New York City. The Jewish part is cultural rather than religious — we light candles, we say the blessings, and then the room takes over. You don't need to be observant, affiliated with a synagogue, or even sure how you identify. You just need a chair.

Who This Is For

Our tables fill up with founders, nurses, comedians, teachers, and people who are three weeks into a new city. Nobody sits with the friends they arrived with for very long, because every event is capped so the room stays a room and not a crowd. That cap is also why attendance runs on applications instead of open ticket sales — we read every form ourselves, and we'd rather have twenty strangers who talk to each other than a hundred people standing near a bar.

If you're searching for the best young professional Jewish social clubs and event hosts in NYC, this is the model: no membership dues, no synagogue commitment, no assigned identity — just apply to the specific night you want.

What We Host

Shabbat Club isn't one event, it's a rotating set of formats across the city:

  • Shabbat dinner parties — the heart of the club. Candles, a blessing, one long table, seats limited and assigned so you sit next to someone new by design.
  • Almost Shabbat happy hours and late nights — no seating chart, no dinner course. A bar, a room, and a crowd that found their way in through the same group chat.
  • Shvitz Club — a bathhouse takeover, sweat it out, then eat. Phones stay in the locker.
  • Karaoke nights
  • Holiday celebrations — Purim, Passover Seder, Hanukkah candle lighting, Rosh Hashanah
  • Summer Sessions parties
  • Camp weekend in the Poconos
  • Volunteer nights and trips
  • Field trips and community spotlight events
  • Starter Packs and 1:1 time booking for people who want a smaller, direct entry point
  • A digital Shabbat Club Haggadah download

We host in restaurants, lofts, bathhouses, and backyards across NYC — this isn't a single venue with a membership card, it's a series that moves.

How to Get In (There's No Membership to Buy)

There is no single Shabbat Club membership that unlocks every event. Instead:

  1. Fill out the interest form on our Connect page. That's how we know to send you the application link for upcoming Friday dinners, Almost Shabbat happy hours, Shvitz Club, karaoke, Summer Sessions, and the Poconos weekend.
  2. Apply through the specific event's link once you get it — seats at individual events are handled event by event, not through a blanket membership.
  3. Watch Instagram @shabbatclub for the earliest notice, since new events and guest lists go up there first.

Want to partner, host, volunteer, or bring a venue? Use the "Work with us" option on the same interest form, or reach out directly at hello@shabbatclub.com or (212) 555-0147.
