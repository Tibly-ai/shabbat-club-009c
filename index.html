What this is

A culturally Jewish pop-up dinner party series and community in NYC.

We set the table on Friday nights and something happens between the first pour and the last plate: strangers turn into a regular crowd.

Shabbat Club runs dinner parties, happy hours, holiday celebrations, karaoke nights, summer parties and a camp weekend upstate. Some rooms hold twenty people. Some hold a few hundred. All of them are built for the same thing: showing up as you are, Jewish however you're Jewish, and leaving with new numbers in your phone.

Seats are limited and every gathering has its own guest list. To attend, apply through the individual event link. We read every application and build each room by hand.

The programming

A year of Fridays, and everything around them.

01

Shabbat dinner parties

The heart of the club. Challah, wine, candles and a seating chart made on purpose. Themed dinners like Safta's Table, Love Club and A Very Schmaltzy Shabbat Club run all year long.

02

Almost Shabbat & karaoke

Thursday-shaped energy. Almost Shabbat happy hours take over a bar or a restaurant, and Shabbat Club Karaoke hands you the mic whether or not you can hold a note.

03

Shvitz Club

Steam, cold plunge, conversation. An old Jewish New York ritual, brought back for a group that mostly met over dinner. The Dry Jan edition is a club favorite.

04

Camp weekend in the Poconos

Wet Hot Hebrew Summer: three days out of the city, Sept 4–6. Bunks, a lake, Friday night dinner under the trees. Applications are open now.
