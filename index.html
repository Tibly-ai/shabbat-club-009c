The programming

5 ways to spend your night with us.

01

Friday night dinner

A full Shabbat night out: cocktails and mingling, candles, challah and wine, followed by a seated dinner with assigned tables. We choose the setting, shape the menu and arrange the tables...and when the space allows, stay late for music and dancing.

02

Almost Shabbat

Thursday-night mixers at Jewish-owned bars, restaurants and creative spaces around the city. Expect drinks, culturally Jewish bites and a casual standing-room format made for moving around and meeting people.

03

The Holidays

Seated dinners and larger parties built around the Jewish calendar, from Rosh Hashanah and Passover to Purim and Hanukkah. Each gathering incorporates the holiday's rituals and food while keeping the night approachable, social and distinctly Shabbat Club.

04

Shvitz Club

A sauna party built around an old Jewish New York ritual: getting sweaty and catching up.

05

Trips & weekends away

Sometimes Shabbat Club takes us beyond the city. Summer camp weekends in the Poconos, volunteer trips in Israel, baseball games, museum tours, and other experiences give us more time together in different places.
