Recognition

Morgan Raum, named to the New York Jewish Week's 36 to Watch, 2024.

The list highlights young Jewish New Yorkers shaping the city's Jewish life. Morgan was included for building Shabbat Club into a room where unaffiliated Jewish adults actually want to spend a Friday night: candles lit, phones down, seventy new people at one long table.

The coverage below spans national business press, Jewish media, television news and independent newsletters. Different angles, same story: Fridays are for friends.
