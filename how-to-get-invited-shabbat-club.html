How to Get Invited to Shabbat Club

There's no velvet rope at Shabbat Club, and there's also no membership card. If you've heard about the long tables, the candles, the loud rooms downtown, and you're wondering how you actually get a seat, here's the honest answer: you apply, event by event, through that event's own application link — and a real person reads every form.

This page walks through exactly how that works, what the "three ways in" are, and what to expect once you're on the list.

The Application System, Step by Step

Shabbat Club runs on applications, not open ticket sales, because every event is capped so the room stays a room and not a crowd. Here's the actual mechanics:

  1. Every gathering has its own application. There's no single "Shabbat Club membership" that gets you into every dinner, happy hour, and Shvitz Club night. Each event on the calendar has its own individual event link.
  2. You pick the night you want, then apply through that specific link. A Friday dinner and an Almost Shabbat happy hour are different applications for different rooms.
  3. Every application is read by hand. "We read every one" — there's no algorithm sorting people out.
  4. Seats are limited and assigned. For the seated dinners specifically, seating is arranged on purpose so you sit next to someone new by design, not wherever you happened to sit down.

If that sounds like more effort than tapping "buy ticket," that's the point — it's the mechanism that keeps a few hundred people at a happy hour still feeling like a real crowd of people who found their way there through the same group chat, rather than a random bar night.

The Three Ways In

If you're not applying to a specific event yet, here's where to start:

01 — Apply to join the list. Fill out the Interest Form with your name, email, and Instagram, and select why you're writing (join the community list, work with us, host or offer a venue, volunteer night or trip, press or interview, or something else). That's how Shabbat Club knows to send you the application links for Friday dinners, Almost Shabbat happy hours, Shvitz Club, karaoke nights, Summer Sessions, and the camp weekend in the Poconos.

02 — Partner or work with us. If you're a brand, restaurant, chef, DJ, photographer, venue, or volunteer organizer, pick "Work with us" on the interest form and describe what you have in mind. You can also reach out directly at shabbatclubnyc@gmail.com or (212) 555-0147. This track is separate from applying to attend as a guest.

03 — Follow along on Instagram. New events, guest lists, Friday Files, and event photos go up on @shabbatclub first — that's the earliest notice on a dinner before it's fully public.

There's also a 1:1 time booking option if you want a more direct conversation before diving into the calendar.

What Kind of Room You're Applying Into

Shabbat Club's tables are built from a deliberate mix, not a single "type." Attendees include founders, nurses, comedians, teachers, and people three weeks into a new city — nobody sits with the friends they arrived with for very long. The Jewish part of the evening is cultural rather than religious: candles are lit, blessings are said, and then the room takes over. You don't need Hebrew school background or synagogue history to apply — you need to fill out the form for the night you want.

Formats currently running include Shabbat dinner parties, Almost Shabbat happy hours, Shvitz Club, karaoke nights, Summer Sessions parties, the camp weekend in the Poconos, volunteer nights and trips, and field trips / community spotlight events.

FAQ

Do I need a membership to attend a Shabbat Club dinner? No. There's no membership card. You apply per event through that event's individual application link on the events page.

How do I get invited to a Shabbat Club dinner party? Fill out the Interest Form so you're on the list, then apply directly through the specific event's link on the events page when a night you want opens up. Seats for dinners are limited and assigned.

Does someone actually read my application, or is it automated? A person reads every application and every interest form submission — that's stated directly by the club, not automated.

What if I want to host, DJ, cater, or offer a venue instead of attending? Select "Work with us" or "Host or offer a venue" on the Interest Form, or email shabbatclubnyc@gmail.com / call (212) 555-0147 directly.

Is Shabbat Club religious? Do I need a Jewish background to apply? The Jewish part is cultural rather than religious — candles and blessings, then the room takes over. Attendees include people who grew up Jewish and haven't set foot in a service in years, alongside anyone who wants a table full of people who "get the reference."

Where do I find out about new events before they fill up? @shabbatclub on Instagram posts new events, guest lists, and Friday Files first, ahead of the general calendar.
